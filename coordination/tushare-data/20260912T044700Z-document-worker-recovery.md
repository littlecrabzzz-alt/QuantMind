- `quantmind-tushare-document-worker` had been left stopped after a clean warm shutdown on September 10. Starting the existing container drained 536 expired dispatch messages without executing them, then accepted a fresh task from the normal acquisition pipeline.
- The first fresh task reached the 110-second hard limit. Its 90-second work loop was followed by an unindexed full-table status grouping over the 1.8 GiB, 1.2-million-row document queue; the status query alone took about 29 seconds. The container remained healthy with restart zero and no OOM.
- Commit `25d81508` adds schema version 3 and the covering index `document_status_counts(download_status,parse_status)`. Sixty-five document tests, Ruff, compileall and diff checks passed. Beat stopped first, the active normal task completed naturally, both workers were stopped, and the production migration completed in 25.258 seconds without revoke or kill.
- The live query now takes 0.119 seconds and its query plan uses the covering index. After exact service restoration, task `f1e48302…` completed with Celery SUCCESS in 90.613 seconds, advancing 40 downloads and 37 parses. Pending documents fell from 1,218,717 to 1,218,679 and parsed documents rose from 20,456 to 20,490.
- Normal worker, document worker and Beat are healthy with restart zero and no OOM. Expanded storage reports 337,107,091,456 free bytes and healthy headroom above the 100 GiB reserve. The approximately 1.22 million pending public documents remain a long-running storage workload, not a completed corpus.
